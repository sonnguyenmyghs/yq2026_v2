Your Christmas Table, Sorted: Inside The Santa Shop 2026

Roast turkey, glazed ham, panettone and hampers for gifting — everything our chefs are cooking for the season, how pre-ordering works, and the early bird codes worth knowing about.

By The Shop Editors12 Nov 20266 min read

Every year the kitchen at Grand Hyatt Singapore starts planning Christmas in late summer. By November the menu is set, the hampers are packed and The Santa Shop opens online, so you can take the hotel’s festive table home without cooking a thing. Here is what is on it this year, and how to order without the last-minute rush.

The roasts

The centrepiece is a herb-roasted Tom turkey of around 5 to 5.5kg, enough for a table of eight to ten. It is joined by a honey garlic butter glazed ham of about 1.6kg, and for beef lovers a garlic pepper crusted Shimo Wagyu MB4/5 rib. Each roast comes with its own sauces and gravies, ready to reheat.

Bundles for every table size

If you would rather not build a menu yourself, the bundles do it for you:

  • Festive Bundle (serves 4) — SGD 388
  • Thanksgiving Bundle (serves 6–8) — SGD 388
  • Family Classics Bundle (serves 6–8) — SGD 488
  • Sweet Treats Bundle — SGD 158, for a dessert-only spread

Cakes, breads and sweets

The pastry team bakes a chestnut clementine log cake, a brandy fruit cake, a traditional Christmas pudding and a signature homemade panettone. There is also a gingerbread house for anyone with children at home, and a 25-piece box of festive pralines for the coffee table.

Hampers for gifting

For colleagues, clients and family you won’t see over the holidays, the Be Merry Hamper and the Festive Shop Hamper mix wine, pralines, cookies and pantry treats. Hampers can be collected from The Shop or sent with a personalised message.

How to order

  1. Choose your items in The Santa Shop and add them to your cart.
  2. Pick a self-collection date at least 48 hours ahead. The Shop is open for collection 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM daily, at the Lobby Level.
  3. Apply your bank’s promo code at checkout if it applies, then pay online.

Early Bird, 6 – 22 Nov 2026: 20% off The Santa Shop with DBS/POSB (code DBSFESTIVE20) or Citibank cards (code CITIFESTIVE20). All festive items need 48 hours’ notice.

Questions about an order? Call +65 6416 7117 or email theshop.sg@hyatt.com.
