About The Shop

The Shop offers a curated selection of cakes, tarts, pastries, cookies, pralines, hampers, wines and beverages, as well as selected dining, wellness and fitness experiences and vouchers.

The Shop is located at the Lobby Level of Grand Hyatt Singapore, offering freshly baked pastries, chocolates, premium produce, beverages and other gourmet selections suitable for grand celebrations, intimate gatherings or solo indulgence.

For assistance with your order, please contact us at +65 6416 7117 or email theshop.sg@hyatt.com.

Ordering

Simply browse our online selection, choose your preferred items, add them to your cart and complete payment online.

Pre-order requirements vary by product. Selected products require at least 48 hours’ advance notice, so we recommend checking the individual product details before completing your purchase.

All products are subject to availability. Certain items may also be seasonal or available only during selected promotional periods.

Yes. The online checkout provides an option to send your purchase as a gift and enter the recipient’s name and email address.

Cakes & Pastries

Yes. The Shop offers a selection of handcrafted cakes, tarts and baked goods prepared by our culinary team. Current selections can be viewed under Bakeries & Cakes on our online shop.

Yes. Our whole cakes and artisanal tarts are suitable for birthdays, anniversaries and other celebrations. Please refer to the individual product page for ordering requirements and availability.

Yes, our cakes, tarts, and baked goods require 48 hours’ advance notice for pre-orders. For urgent requests, please visit The Shop to view the range of pastries available for purchase in-store on the day.

Where available, allergen information is provided within individual product descriptions. If you have a specific food allergy or dietary requirement, please contact The Shop before placing your order so our team can assist you.

Self-Collection

Yes. Self-collection is available daily from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

Orders for self-collection can be collected from The Shop at Grand Hyatt Singapore, Lobby Level.

Orders not collected on the selected collection date are considered fulfilled and cannot be exchanged for replacement goods.

Delivery

Yes. Delivery is available during selected time slots: 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM, 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM and 5:00 PM – 7:00 PM. Availability may depend on the product ordered.

Yes. Complimentary one-time delivery to one location in Singapore is available for orders of SGD 300 or more on a single receipt, subject to the applicable terms and conditions.

Delivery times may be affected by weather, road conditions or other unforeseen circumstances.

A failed delivery may be subject to an additional delivery fee depending on the revised delivery location.

Payment, Changes & Cancellations

Orders must be paid in full in advance and placed according to the required processing time for the selected item.

Yes. Prices displayed on The Shop are nett and inclusive of prevailing government taxes.

Orders are non-cancellable and non-refundable once confirmed. We therefore recommend reviewing your order carefully before completing payment.

Confirmed delivery dates and times cannot be changed. Please ensure your preferred delivery details are correct before completing your purchase.

World of Hyatt

World of Hyatt points currently cannot be redeemed through this e-commerce platform.

Eligibility may vary depending on the purchase. The published terms specifically state that alcohol purchases are not eligible to earn World of Hyatt points. For other purchases, please contact The Shop if you would like to confirm eligibility before ordering.

Dining, Wellness & Experiences

Yes. Selected dining experiences and vouchers are available through The Shop’s online platform.

Yes, selected DAMAI wellness experiences, including massages, facials, yoga programmes and other wellness offerings, are available for purchase through our online shop. For enquiries about specific wellness experiences, please contact the DAMAI team at +65 6416 7132 or email damai.sg@hyatt.com.

Yes, selected gift vouchers and experiences are available for purchase online and make the perfect gift for celebrations and special occasions. Explore a curated selection of dining experiences, wellness and spa treatments, fitness memberships and exclusive hotel offerings, designed to create memorable moments at Grand Hyatt Singapore.
