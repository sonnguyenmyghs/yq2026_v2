Images use relative paths (../../assets/img/…) for local review only. For a real test send, every image (banner, logo, QR) must be an absolute https:// URL.
Overlay and split point at the original 1200×900 photos; in production the backend must pre-crop them (overlay exactly 592×460, split 416×600) — email clients cannot crop. QR here is a sample SVG; the real %qr_code% should be a PNG/JPG <img> (~120px), since many clients block SVG.
